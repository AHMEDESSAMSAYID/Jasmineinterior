STEEL

Restaurant & hotel fit-out

Fitting out a restaurant or a hotel is not buying domestic furniture: every piece is used dozens of times a day and cleaned with strong agents, and a failure stops trading rather than merely looking bad. So in this category we work with factories that already produce for the Turkish hospitality market — with a made-to-measure line and a project record you can go and see — not domestic furniture makers who sell to restaurants on the side.

What this area covers

The items we supply in this area

The list below shows what we handle regularly; it is not exhaustive. If the item you are after is not listed, send it to us and we will go and find its factory on the ground.

FURNITURE

Restaurant and café furniture

Chairs, tables, banquette seating and table bases. What separates a chair that lasts one year from one that lasts five is the frame, the foam density and the fabric — not the shape. We ask for foam density as a figure and fabric abrasion resistance stated in the offer, because “strong fabric” is not a specification anyone can be held to.

STAINLESS

Fixed stainless steel equipment

Work benches, sinks, shelving, extraction hoods and trolleys. These are built to the kitchen's dimensions rather than to stock sizes, so we ask for a drawing or exact measurements before pricing. Surfaces are usually 1.2 to 1.5 mm in grade 304 where they meet food, and we measure the thickness ourselves during inspection because it is the line item most quietly reduced.

BEDDING

Hotel bedding and linen

Mattresses and protectors, duvets, pillows and sheets — as complete sets for the room count, with a replacement ratio worked out for the operating cycle. A contract-grade mattress differs from a domestic one in its foam layers, its spring system and how it holds up to continuous daily use.

Before we start

What we need from you, and what we check on your behalf

What we need to start pricing

The clearer the request, the sharper the shortlist and the closer the offer to what you actually want.

  • Number of covers or rooms
  • A floor plan or exact dimensions
  • The style you want, with reference images if you have them
  • Your target opening date
  • Whether you need logo embroidery on the linen
  • Approximate budget per unit

What we check before loading

An on-site inspection of the actual goods, and a photographic report in your hands before the container leaves the factory.

  • Chairs and tables tested under load
  • Weld quality and polishing on stainless pieces
  • Stainless thickness measured, not taken from the offer
  • Foam density against what the purchase order specifies
  • Fabric colour fastness and match to the sample
  • Embroidered logo against the approved artwork
  • Full count per room before loading
