Finishes & hardware

The pieces that set how a space feels: lighting, doors, handles and tiling. They are usually the last things ordered on a project and the first to run late, and they are also the items that fail over small technical details — a different voltage, a door hung the wrong way, or a second tile batch one shade lighter than the first.

What this area covers

The items we supply in this area

The list below shows what we handle regularly; it is not exhaustive. If the item you are after is not listed, send it to us and we will go and find its factory on the ground.

LIGHTING

Decorative lighting

Chandeliers, pendants, linear fittings and spots. We fix the voltage, the lamp base and the colour temperature before pricing, and ask for a sample to be powered up before the quantity is approved. A fitting with the wrong voltage or base is not a defect to be repaired — it is a shipment that cannot be used.

DOORS

Doors and entrance units

Internal doors, security doors and glazed doors. A door is built to the opening rather than to a stock size, so we need the clear opening, the wall thickness and the swing direction before manufacture — and the swing in particular cannot be reversed afterwards on most types.

HARDWARE

Metal hardware

Handles, hinges, locks and drawer runners. Small in price and large in how a space feels day to day, and ordered in volume — so a fraction of a lira becomes a real difference, and finish consistency between batches becomes worth fixing in writing.

TILES

Ceramic and porcelain tiling

Porcelain and ceramic in a range of formats for walls and floors. We order the full quantity from a single production batch, because a batch difference reads as a clear colour line across a floor, and we ask for the slip resistance rating to be stated in the offer for wet areas and public spaces.

Before we start

What we need from you, and what we check on your behalf

What we need to start pricing

The clearer the request, the sharper the shortlist and the closer the offer to what you actually want.

  • Quantities per item, with a wastage allowance for tiling
  • Clear opening sizes and wall thickness for doors
  • The swing direction of each door
  • Voltage and lamp base for lighting
  • The setting: residential, commercial or a wet area
  • Approximate budget

What we check before loading

An on-site inspection of the actual goods, and a photographic report in your hands before the container leaves the factory.

  • Single production batch for tiling, with the batch number recorded
  • Lighting samples actually powered up before packing
  • Door and frame sizes against the clear opening
  • Fixing accessories complete with every piece
  • Finish consistency across hardware pieces
  • Packing adequate for breakable items
