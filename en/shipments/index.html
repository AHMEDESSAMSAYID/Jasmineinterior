Shipment record

Our shipments

We document every shipment with photographs and the item and destination details. This section is updated with each operation, so there is a record you can go back to rather than general promises.

We do not publish our clients' shipments here

Every shipment is documented with photographs and the item and destination details, but publishing that openly reveals what our clients buy and from where — and that is theirs, not ours. Ask us for photographs of past shipments in an area close to yours and we will send them to you directly, along with an actual inspection report, with anything identifying the client removed.

What we document

What this record holds

Every shipment has a file we keep and share a copy of with you, and what the client approves is published here.

Shipment details

Information describing the shipment without disclosing anything about the client or their pricing.

  • The supply area and the main items
  • Approximate quantity and container count
  • Port of loading and destination port
  • Loading date and arrival date
  • The factory's city in Türkiye

Photographic documentation

The same photographs the client receives before the load is released.

  • Images from the production line inside the factory
  • The goods packed before loading
  • Packing and how the load is stowed
  • The inspection report with our notes
  • The container before it is sealed
