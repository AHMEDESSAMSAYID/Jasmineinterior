About us

A buying office, not a commission broker

Jasmine Interiors & Fit-Out is a division of Jasmine Import & Export, based in Istanbul. Our work is to stand in your place inside the Turkish factory: to search, to negotiate, to inspect, and to carry responsibility for what we put in front of you.

The short version

We started from a problem we know from both sides

We spent years exporting from Türkiye to Arab markets, and saw the same scene repeat: an importer pays up front against photographs, then discovers on arrival that what came is not what was agreed. The problem is rarely the factory's intent — it is that nobody stood inside it to check.

So we decided that would be precisely our work: to be your eyes in the factory. We do not sell you goods from our own stock, and we do not take a hidden margin on the factory price. We work on a commission agreed in advance, and our interest is in you coming back rather than in winning once.

What we are

Our role is defined and clear from the first message.

  • A buying office working on your behalf inside Türkiye
  • We find the factory and negotiate in Turkish
  • We inspect production on site before loading
  • We follow documents and shipping to the destination port
  • We work on a commission agreed in advance

What we are not

Being clear about what we do not do saves everyone time.

  • Not a wholesaler selling you from our own stock
  • We take no hidden margin on the factory price
  • We do not shortlist a factory we have not visited
  • We give no verbal or estimated prices
  • We do not handle customs clearance at destination
